Severity escalation policy

Sanctions escalate based on the number and severity of infractions. Repeated offenses move to the next level automatically.

Warning
Probation
Suspension

Warning: First-time or minor infractions. A formal notice.

Probation: Repeated warnings or moderate violations. Restrictions may apply.

Suspension: Severe or repeated probation-level offenses. Temporary removal from events.
